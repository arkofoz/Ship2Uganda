Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about shipping with us.

How long does shipping from UAE to Uganda take?

Typically 14-21 days for sea freight, 5-7 days for air freight. CBM shipments take 21-30 days depending on consolidation.

What is CBM and how is it calculated?

CBM stands for Cubic Meter, the volume of your cargo. It's calculated as Length × Width × Height in meters. For large shipments, we charge by CBM rather than weight.

Do you ship from Amazon UAE only or other stores?

We ship from any UAE store including Amazon, Noon, Carrefour, and local shops. Just send us the link and we'll handle the rest.

How do I track my shipment?

We provide manual tracking via WhatsApp. You'll receive updates at every stage—when received, shipped, and arrived in Uganda.

What are your shipping rates?

Rates vary by source and weight. Use our calculator above for estimates. Final quotes are confirmed by human review.

Do you offer insurance for shipments?

Yes, we offer optional insurance at 2% of declared value. This covers loss or damage during transit.

What items are prohibited?

We don't ship weapons, illegal drugs, hazardous materials, or counterfeit goods. See our T&Cs for full list.

Can you ship to upcountry destinations?

Absolutely! We deliver to all major towns in Uganda including Jinja, Gulu, Mbarara, Mbale, and more via our inland delivery service.

How do I pay for shipping?

We accept Mobile Money (MTN/Airtel), bank transfer, and cash on pickup. Payment is required before shipping.

What happens if my package is delayed?

We communicate all delays immediately. While we work with reliable carriers, some delays are beyond our control. We'll always keep you updated.
